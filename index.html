About Me

I began my career in the trades, earning my Interprovincial Red Seal endorsement as a Journeyperson Insulator and gaining experience in project supervision, cost estimating, and project controls.

Building on that industry experience, I transitioned into forestry, environmental, regulatory, and geospatial work.

I hold a Forest Technology diploma from NAIT and a Bachelor of Applied Technology in Geographic Information Systems (GIS) from SAIT.

My current work includes regulatory mapping, spatial analysis, field data collection, reclamation support, cartography, and the development of practical browser-based GIS tools.
